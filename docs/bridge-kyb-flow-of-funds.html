TecnoWallet — Flow of Funds

Bridge KYB · Intended use-case: Recaudos (shared group collections). Applicant: TecnoWallet · Software orchestration only · Funds custodied by Bridge, not by TecnoWallet.

1. Currency exchange? Yes, only if needed. If the contributor pays in the same currency as the organizer’s payout (typically USD), there is no FX. If currencies differ (e.g. COP in → USD hold, or USD hold → COP bank), Bridge converts at payout/on-ramp. TecnoWallet does not run its own FX book.
2. Ultimate destination? The Recaudo organizer’s personal bank account (the end user who created the shared pot), when they withdraw after the goal is reached. Recaudo funds never settle into TecnoWallet’s operating account. Subscriptions and affiliate USDT are separate and do not use Bridge.

Primary flow — Recaudos via Bridge

1. Contributors

Friends, family, or group members in TecnoWallet. They pledge a monthly or one-time amount to a Recaudo (trip, gift, event, family support, purchase).

→

2. Funding rail

Local bank transfer / ACH / Bridge on-ramp into USD (or USDC). TecnoWallet records the contribution against the Recaudo; it does not take possession of the cash.

→

3. Bridge account

A Bridge virtual account / wallet in the organizer’s name, tagged to that Recaudo. Funds sit here until the group goal is met. Partner custody: Bridge.

→

4. Organizer bank

Ultimate destination. Organizer withdraws to their own bank (USD wire or local off-ramp, e.g. COP). Purpose: pay the trip, gift, event, or family need — not for resale of payments.

TecnoWallet fee on the pot: none. Bridge/network fees may apply as charged by Bridge. TecnoWallet’s role: create the Recaudo, invite members, show balances, trigger payout to the organizer.

Intended use-case (narrative)

TecnoWallet is a personal-finance app. Recaudos let a user (the organizer) create a shared pot: “we are saving US$2,000 for a trip”, “siblings sending money to our parents”, “group gift”. Each member has a contribution plan. When Bridge is enabled, TecnoWallet opens a digital account in the organizer’s name via Bridge. Members fund that account. When the goal is reached (or on an allowed withdrawal), the organizer sends the balance to their own bank account.

TecnoWallet is not a marketplace, not a payroll product, and not a crypto exchange. It does not pool recaudo funds with other customers in a TecnoWallet omnibus for its own use. It does not exchange recaudo funds as a dealer; any FX is Bridge converting the same customer’s money from the contribution currency into the payout currency.

Today vs. intended. Recaudos already exist in the app as group tracking. Until Bridge APIs are approved, contributions are recorded as accounting only (members pay the organizer off-platform). This application is to enable the real movement: inbound funding → Bridge-held recaudo account → outbound to the organizer’s bank.
